PART B — GENERAL CONDITIONS
Note: The following General Conditions form an integral part of this contract. They are intended to be read together with the Special Conditions (Part A). In the event of any inconsistency between the Special Conditions and the General Conditions, the Special Conditions shall prevail. These General Conditions reflect the standard provisions of the ICC Model International Sale Contract (Manufactured Goods) — 2020 Edition.

Article 1 — General

  1. 1.1 These General Conditions are intended to be applied together with the Special Conditions (Part A) of the ICC Model International Sale Contract for Manufactured Goods. In the event of any contradiction between these General Conditions and the Special Conditions, the Special Conditions shall prevail.
  2. 1.2 Any questions relating to this contract which are not expressly or impliedly settled by the provisions contained in the contract itself (i.e. these General Conditions and the Special Conditions) shall be governed:
    (a) by the United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods (Vienna Convention of 1980, hereinafter referred to as "CISG"), and
    (b) to the extent not covered by CISG, by reference to the law applicable at the Seller's place of business, unless otherwise agreed in Special Condition A-14.
  3. 1.3 Any reference to trade terms (such as EXW, FCA, etc.) is understood as a reference to the relevant Incoterms® 2020 rule.
  4. 1.4 Any reference to a publication of the International Chamber of Commerce is to the version current at the date of conclusion of the contract.
  5. 1.5 No modification of this contract is valid unless agreed or evidenced in writing. "Writing" includes authenticated electronic communications.

Article 2 — Characteristics of the Goods

  1. 2.1 It is agreed that the Seller shall deliver goods which conform with the description in Special Condition A-4, and that the goods must possess the qualities that the Seller has held out to the Buyer as a sample or model.
  2. 2.2 Unless otherwise agreed, the goods must also be fit for the purposes for which goods of the same description would ordinarily be used, be contained or packaged in the manner usual for such goods, and possess the qualities necessary for any particular purpose expressly or impliedly made known to the Seller.

Article 3 — Delivery

  1. 3.1 Unless otherwise agreed in Special Condition A-7, delivery shall be made within 30 days from the date of conclusion of the contract or within 30 days of the Seller's receipt of notice that an agreed documentary credit or other payment security has been issued in Seller's favour.
  2. 3.2 The Seller shall provide the documents specified in Special Condition A-11 (or if none are specified, those required by the applicable Incoterms® rule).
  3. 3.3 If pre-shipment inspection has been agreed upon (Special Condition A-8), the Seller shall notify the Buyer in due time that the goods are ready for inspection.

Article 4 — Price and Payment

  1. 4.1 Unless otherwise agreed in Special Condition A-10, the Buyer shall pay the price by placing an irrevocable payment order with the Buyer's bank, within 30 days of the date of invoice, by electronic transfer to the Seller's bank account specified in the invoice or in Special Condition A-10.
  2. 4.2 If the parties have agreed on payment by documentary credit and unless otherwise agreed, the Buyer shall arrange for an irrevocable documentary credit in favour of the Seller, subject to the ICC Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits (UCP 600), to be issued at least 30 days before the agreed date of delivery or the earliest date within the agreed delivery period.
  3. 4.3 If the Buyer fails to pay any amount due under the contract by the due date, the Seller shall be entitled to charge interest on the outstanding amount from the due date until payment at a rate of 2% above the average short-term lending rate for the currency of payment at the place of Seller's business, or if no such rate is available, at the appropriate rate in the country of the Seller's currency of payment.
  4. 4.4 The Buyer shall not be entitled to set-off or make deductions from the price without the Seller's written consent, unless the Buyer's right to set-off or deduction has been established by a final court award or arbitral award or has been acknowledged by the Seller.

Article 5 — Contractual Non-Conformity

  1. 5.1 The Buyer shall examine the goods, or cause them to be examined, within as short a period as is practicable after arrival at destination.
  2. 5.2 The Buyer shall notify the Seller specifying the nature of the non-conformity within a reasonable time after the Buyer has discovered or ought to have discovered the non-conformity, but in any event not later than 14 days from arrival of the goods at destination.
  3. 5.3 In any case, the Buyer loses the right to rely on a lack of conformity if the Buyer fails to notify the Seller within 24 months of the date of arrival of the goods at destination, unless modified in Special Condition A-17.

Article 6 — Remedies for Non-Conformity

  1. 6.1 Unless the non-conformity is such that it would be unreasonable to expect the Buyer to accept the goods, the Seller shall, at its option and without undue delay, repair or replace the defective goods free of charge or grant a reduction in price.
  2. 6.2 If the non-conformity is fundamental, the Buyer may claim damages, or, alternatively, declare the contract avoided with respect to the defective goods.

Article 7 — Transfer of Property / Retention of Title

  1. 7.1 Unless retention of title has been agreed in Special Condition A-9, property in the goods shall pass to the Buyer at the time of delivery, as determined by the applicable Incoterms® rule.
  2. 7.2 Where retention of title has been agreed, the goods remain the property of the Seller until complete payment of the price or as otherwise agreed.

Article 8 — Seller's Liability for Damage Caused by the Goods

The Seller shall be liable for damage caused by the goods to persons or property after delivery, only if the damage is due to the Seller's fault. The Seller's liability for such damage shall not exceed the contract price, unless otherwise agreed in Special Condition A-13.

Article 9 — Warranty Period

The Seller's obligations under these General Conditions in respect of lack of conformity of the goods (including latent defects) are limited to defects which are notified to the Seller within 24 months from delivery, or such other period as may be agreed in Special Condition A-17.

Article 10 — Late Performance and Non-Performance

  1. 10.1 Where there is delay in delivery that does not amount to non-performance under Art. 10.3, the Buyer is entitled to claim liquidated damages as agreed in Special Condition A-12, or, if no liquidated damages have been agreed, to claim proven damages.
  2. 10.2 Liquidated damages for delay, if agreed, shall accrue for each complete week of delay from the delivery date specified in the contract. They shall not exceed the maximum stated in Special Condition A-12.
  3. 10.3 If a party fails to perform any of its obligations and does not cure its failure within a reasonable additional period granted by the other party, the aggrieved party may terminate the contract by notice.

Article 11 — Limitation of Liability

  1. 11.1 Except as otherwise provided in these General Conditions, the Seller's liability for any breach of contract shall be limited to the foreseeable loss and shall not exceed the total contract price.
  2. 11.2 Except in cases of gross negligence or wilful misconduct, the Seller shall in no event be liable for any indirect, consequential, or incidental damages, including but not limited to loss of profit, loss of business, or loss of goodwill.
  3. 11.3 These limitations apply to the Seller's liability in contract, tort, or otherwise.

Article 12 — Force Majeure

  1. 12.1 A party is not liable for a failure to perform any of its obligations insofar as it proves that the failure was due to an impediment beyond its reasonable control, that it could not reasonably have been expected to take the impediment into account at the time of the conclusion of the contract, and that it could not reasonably have avoided or overcome the impediment or its effects.
  2. 12.2 A party seeking relief shall notify the other party of the impediment and its effect on its ability to perform without delay. The parties shall use reasonable endeavours to mitigate the effects of the force majeure event.
  3. 12.3 If the impediment or its effects last for more than 6 months (or such other period agreed by the parties), either party may terminate the contract by notice.

Article 13 — Hardship

  1. 13.1 Where the performance of the contract becomes substantially more onerous for one of the parties due to an event beyond its reasonable control which it could not reasonably have been expected to have taken into account at the time of conclusion of the contract, and where that party has not assumed the risk of such changed circumstances, the parties are bound to negotiate alternative contractual terms which reasonably allow for the consequences of the event.
  2. 13.2 If the parties fail to reach agreement within a reasonable time, either party may resort to the dispute resolution mechanism agreed in Special Condition A-15.

Article 14 — Dispute Resolution

  1. 14.1 Unless otherwise agreed in Special Condition A-15, all disputes arising out of or in connection with this contract shall be finally settled under the Rules of Arbitration of the International Chamber of Commerce by one or more arbitrators appointed in accordance with the said Rules.
  2. 14.2 Nothing in this article shall prevent either party from seeking interim or conservatory measures from any court of competent jurisdiction.

Article 15 — Miscellaneous

  1. 15.1 Entire Agreement: This contract (Special Conditions, General Conditions, and Annexes) constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and agreements relating to the subject matter of this contract.
  2. 15.2 Severability: If any provision of this contract is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.
  3. 15.3 Assignment: Neither party may assign its rights or obligations under this contract without the prior written consent of the other party, except that the Seller may assign its right to receive payment.
  4. 15.4 Notices: All notices and communications under this contract shall be in writing and sent to the addresses specified in Special Conditions A-2 and A-3, or to such other address as a party may designate by notice.
  5. 15.5 Waiver: No failure or delay by either party in exercising any right under this contract shall operate as a waiver thereof.
